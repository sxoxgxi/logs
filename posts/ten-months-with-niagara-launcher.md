# Ten Months with Niagara Launcher

**June 17, 2026**

Lately I've been thinking about how long it's been since I switched to Niagara Launcher.

Funny enough, I found it while scrolling Twitter one evening when I was in one of those moods where I wanted to change *something* but had no idea what. A new launcher seemed harmless enough.

That was around ten months ago.

Most apps I get excited about end up disappearing after a week or two. This one somehow stuck.

The thing I noticed right away was how calm it felt. My home screen is basically just the apps I use most, nothing fighting for attention. Sometimes it almost looks like a lockscreen, which I weirdly like.

Getting around is fast, too. A quick swipe along the side and I'm where I need to be. After a while it became muscle memory. Going back to a traditional launcher now feels a little crowded.

I even ended up making a custom wallpaper for it. Nothing fancy, but it ties everything together and still makes me smile when I unlock my phone.

What's surprising is that I don't really have any complaints after all this time. No major annoyances, no "I'll switch next month" phase. It's just quietly doing its job every day.

I guess that's why I'm still using it.

It's one of those small changes that doesn't seem important at first, but somehow makes a thing you use hundreds of times a day feel a little nicer.

Makes me wonder what other tiny tweaks are out there waiting to become part of my routine.
