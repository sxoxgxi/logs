# Small Tasks, Actually Getting Done

**March 24, 2026**

I’ve been kind of drowning in small things lately.

Not big, scary problems. Just… a lot of tiny unfinished stuff. College assignments stacking up, GitHub issues sitting there quietly judging me, random ideas showing up at 1am like they pay rent, chores I keep forgetting for no good reason.

It all started slipping through the cracks.

Then one random burst of “okay, fix your life” energy hit me. I almost started building my own app to solve the problem, but stopped halfway through the thought. Felt a bit stupid, honestly. I already live inside Google anyway, so I just opened Google Tasks.

First time actually using it.

And yeah… it just worked.

Nothing fancy. Just deadlines, repeating tasks, little subtasks when something feels too big. And those gentle reminders that don’t yell at you, just… nudge you.

Since then, things have been weirdly smoother. Assignments getting done on time. I finally watched that movie someone told me about weeks ago. GitHub issues are actually closing. PRs aren’t just sitting there anymore.

Even the dumb little things like cleaning the cat’s litter, eating something I planned instead of whatever’s nearby, replying to messages I kept putting off.

It’s not life-changing or anything. Just… quiet progress.

Funny how something this simple can shift things a bit.

Maybe I didn’t need a better system.  
Just one I’d actually use.
